```

steve jobs was right: you only connect the dots looking backward.

AI is making that more obvious than ever. the people winning are not always the ones who learned the newest tool first. they are the ones who spent years accumulating useful dots.

the filmmaker understands pacing, framing, emotion and attention. the writer understands compression, clarity and narrative. the designer understands taste and hierarchy. the engineer understands systems, constraints and tradeoffs.

AI gives all of them new leverage. the tools changed, but the underlying advantage did not.

your old skills are not obsolete. they are becoming inputs into a much larger machine.

the more disciplines you understand, the more unusual connections you can make. and in a world where everyone has access to the same models, those connections become the edge.

```

同样作为产品人，给我的感触最深。

所以我们要看不同领域书籍，参与不同领域具体的工作，尤其是我们 AI 硬件产品。

技术/商业/经济/历史/建筑/艺术等等。
